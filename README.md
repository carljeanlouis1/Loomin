# Loomin

Customer feedback analyzer for small and medium hotels and restaurants, where handling reviews is a one-person job. Built at Pioneering Minds AI: Hack for Impact (AI for Product Management Workflows track).

## Pitch deck

Open `slides/index.html` in Chrome.

- Arrow keys or the on-screen buttons move between slides
- `F` toggles full screen, `N` shows speaker notes
- Slide 5 plays the demo video: press Space

## Demo video

`video/loomin-demo.mp4` is a 1:43 walkthrough of the prototype with voiceover: sign-in, morning brief, what to fix first, sentiment and keyword trends, reply drafts, the "Ask your reviews" chat, social comments, and connecting sources.
